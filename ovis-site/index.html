How it works

Ovis opens the herdr web UI that your own computer serves. Nothing runs in between.

  1. Run herdr web UI on your PC

    It is free and open source. On Linux or macOS one command installs everything; Windows and other ways are on herdrweb.dev.

    curl -fsSL https://herdrweb.dev/install.sh | sh

  2. Pair your phone

    On the PC, open Settings → Phone & devices and scan its QR code with Ovis, or type the address it shows.

  3. Answer from anywhere

    Approvals and questions arrive as cards you tap. The agent's own terminal is one tap away, with a key bar for Esc, Tab and Ctrl.

Questions

What is herdr?

herdr is a terminal multiplexer for coding agents: it runs Claude Code, Codex and other agents side by side in panes. herdr web UI brings those panes to a browser, and Ovis brings them to your phone.

Do I need Tailscale?

No, but it is the easiest way. Your phone needs a route to your PC: Tailscale, a VPN, the same Wi-Fi, or your own HTTPS proxy. The phone setup guide walks through each.

Is it free?

Yes. Ovis and herdr web UI are free, and herdr web UI is open source under the MIT license.

Can I try it without a PC?

Yes. The connect screen has a demo with made-up agents and terminals. Nothing leaves your phone.

Which phones?

iPhone and Android. Both apps are in testing and will be in the App Store and Google Play soon.
